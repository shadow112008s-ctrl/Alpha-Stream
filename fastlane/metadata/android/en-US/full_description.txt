Alpha Stream is an Android media-center app for organizing and playing video from sources that users configure.

Features include:

Search and media details

Bookmarks and watch progress

Built-in video playback and subtitle support

Download management for content you are authorized to save

Optional compatible extensions and external-player support

The app does not bundle movie, TV-series, or anime catalogues by default. Availability depends on the sources a user configures. Use only lawful sources and content you are authorized to access.

Some features depend on the configured provider, Android version, and device.
