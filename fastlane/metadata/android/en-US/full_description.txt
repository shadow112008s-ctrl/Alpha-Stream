Alpha Stream is an extension-based multimedia player for Android phones and TV devices.

Discover and organize media through compatible extensions that you choose to install. Alpha Stream does not bundle video sources. Only install extensions and access media that you are authorized to use.

Features:
- Customizable extension-based media playback
- Bookmarks and personal library
- Subtitle support and subtitle downloads
- Chromecast support
- Phone and Android TV layouts
- Offline playback for supported downloads

Availability of media, playback features and extensions depends on the extension you choose and the rights and services available in your region.

Alpha Stream is a media player. It does not provide, host, or endorse third-party media sources.
